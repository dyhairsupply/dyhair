WHO WE ARE

A Direct Manufacturer of Human Virgin Hair

Shandong DY Hair Products Factory is a direct manufacturer of human virgin hair. We run a complete production workflow: raw material sorting, hair processing, hand ventilating, styling, quality inspection and packing.

We select high-quality virgin hair strictly without mixed inferior hair. We focus on lace wigs, lace closures, hair extensions and hair wefts.

We serve buyers at home and abroad and support customized solutions. All photos and videos are real shots without excessive retouching for authentic quality display.

PRODUCTION WORKFLOW

Six In-house Steps From Raw Hair to Finished Goods

Every step is completed in-house, which keeps quality and lead time under control and allows order-specific adjustments.

01

Raw Material Sorting

Grading by texture, length and color; removing substandard strands.

02

Hair Processing

Cleaning, combing and alignment while keeping the natural texture.

03

Hand Ventilating

Skilled workers ventilate strand by strand for a breathable, comfortable cap.

04

Styling

Styling and trimming to the ordered curl pattern and color.

05

Quality Inspection

Checking stitching, shedding, smoothness and odor.

06

Packing & Storage

Sorted storage, individual packing and shipment per order.
