HAIR CARE GUIDE

Proper Care, Longer Lasting Hair

Detangling, washing, drying, storage and mistakes to avoid for lace wigs, closures, extensions, wefts and ponytails.

Home / Hair Care
CARE BASICS

Treat It Like Your Own Hair

Human virgin hair keeps its natural cuticle and can be dyed or restyled, so it needs gentle cleansing and regular care. Proper detangling, washing and storage noticeably reduce tangling, shedding and dryness while preserving smoothness and curl.

01

Detangling

  • Detangle before and after each wear, starting at the ends and working up in sections; never pull from the roots.
  • Use a wide-tooth comb or a wig brush; finger-comb curly styles to preserve the curl pattern.
  • Wet hair stretches easily; separate gently with fingers or a wide-tooth comb without pulling.
02

Washing

  • Wash every 8–15 wears, or when oil and product buildup appear; daily washing is unnecessary.
  • Use lukewarm water (about 30°C) and a mild sulfate-free shampoo; press gently along the hair direction, never rub or wring.
  • Apply conditioner from mid-length to ends only, keeping it off the roots, lace base and bonded tips; rinse thoroughly.
  • Do not soak the lace base for long, as ventilated knots and stitched seams may loosen.
03

Drying

  • Blot with a towel instead of rubbing back and forth, which damages the cuticle and causes tangles.
  • Air-dry on a wig stand, away from direct sunlight and heater vents.
  • Minimize blow-drying; if needed, use low or cool heat at least 15 cm away. Comb, style or store only once fully dry.
04

Storage

  • Store only when fully dry; damp storage can cause odor or mildew.
  • Keep on a wig stand, in a breathable bag or the original box, away from light, dust, pressure and folding.
  • Loosely gather curly styles or use a hair net to hold the curl pattern.
  • For long-term storage, add a desiccant and keep away from heat and humidity.

Heat Styling & Coloring

Virgin hair can be restyled and dyed. Keep tools at 160–180°C or below and apply a heat protectant first; avoid repeated high heat on one section and keep heat away from the lace base, seams and bonds. Bleaching or lightening shortens hair life, so have it done by a professional stylist.

Wearing & Regular Care

Comb and adjust the hairline before wearing, and return the piece to a stand afterwards to recover its shape. Avoid sleeping, swimming or saunas while wearing it. Periodic deep conditioning helps over time; if tangling or shedding increases, consult a professional instead of force-combing.

MISTAKES TO AVOID

What Shortens the Life of Your Hair

Most of these mistakes come from treating a wig like ordinary fabric. Avoiding them is far more effective than repairing damage later.

×

Hot water, or products containing sulfates and alcohol.

×

Yanking from the roots or using a fine-tooth comb on tangles.

×

Rubbing, wringing or towel-drying wet hair back and forth.

×

Applying conditioner or masks to the lace base or bonded tips.

×

Storing while damp, or wearing it while sleeping, swimming or in a sauna.

×

High-heat blow-drying, frequent bleaching, or repeated heat on one section.

×

Direct sun exposure, or storing near heaters and humid bathrooms.

×

Folding or storing under pressure, or leaving it uncombed and unwashed for long.

QUICK REFERENCE

Key Care Parameters

ITEM RECOMMENDATION
Washing Frequency Every 8–15 wears, or when oil and product buildup appear
Water Temperature Lukewarm water at about 30°C; avoid hot water
Products Mild sulfate-free shampoo plus conditioner on mid-lengths and ends
Styling Temperature Keep at or below 160–180°C with a heat protectant
Drying Blot with a towel, then air-dry on a stand away from sunlight
Storage Fully dry, dark, dust-free, breathable and free of pressure

These are general care guidelines for human hair products. Requirements vary by construction such as lace bases, bonded extensions and wefts; always follow the instructions supplied with your product.
